Two different mechanisms, easy to confuse: node **affinity** is the Pod expressing a preference or requirement about the node; a **taint** is the node repelling Pods, and a **toleration** is the Pod's permission slip to ignore a specific taint. Affinity gets you onto the right node; a toleration is what keeps you from being blocked by one you're allowed on.
